# Jean Claude vs. Build

**Both understand you today. But what do they remember tomorrow?**

*Illustrative example: same project, same instruction, two sessions.*

## 1. Today — first session

**You, to both:**

> For this project, commit messages are in Spanish, short, with scope. Remember that.

| 🥋 Jean Claude | 🔧 Standard Build |
|---|---|
| "Noted. I'll remember that for future sessions." | "Got it! Commits will be in Spanish, short, with scope." |
| **Saves** the preference to its memory. | Keeps it in mind during this conversation. |

## 2. Tomorrow — new session

**You, to both:**

> What format do we use for commit messages?

| 🥋 Jean Claude | 🔧 Standard Build |
|---|---|
| "Spanish, short, with scope. For example: `feat(auth): validar email en signup`." | "I don't have what we agreed yesterday available. What format do you prefer?" |
| **Retrieves** what it saved. | The previous session ended; that preference isn't automatically available. |

---

**Jean Claude brings Claude Code's auto-memory approach to OpenCode: saving what matters and retrieving it when you come back.**

*Standard Build can also keep instructions if you write them manually in `AGENTS.md`. Jean Claude's retrieval isn't infallible either.*
